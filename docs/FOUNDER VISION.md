The vision is to create a place
In which we put together what problems in the world need to be solved
This would be done by a similar system as Big Ideas DB or the Idea Browser

I am thinking that this should be possible by putting together arbitrage signals, seeing what already works abroad, then demand - bottom-up signals what the people want (reviews, data exhaust, ), Top-down signals: What do the governments have envisioned and what they want (tenders, grants, diagnostics), and what people are willing to put their money into? The VC and the capital signals: What was funded, and where are people actually putting their money?

This should be done by getting data from various sources, like what startups are being founded in YC Combinator or in other countries.Intersecting this with the problems in a given city, state, or country. And enabling a user, which is enhanced by the AI assistant, to go and look with my unique network, with my unique skills, interests, and my geographical positions, what problems can I actually solve? This place should provide it for him through an easy landing page that scores all the different problems and ideas, and later maybe by a newsletter.

The technical vision is that, from various sources, we normalise the data and put it into some JSONL file. Or to some database, and then we look at how it's relevant for various geographical locations.

The product just looks for various sources and sees what ideas actually have some potential so that someone can solve them. If someone is already solving them on the market or a company moves into a new region, then we should de-rank the idea.
