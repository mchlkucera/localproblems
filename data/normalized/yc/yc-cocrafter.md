- Model: two-sided B2B marketplace for GC-subcontractor matching incl. foreign subcontractor sourcing and document/compliance management; proven in the culturally similar German SMB construction sector.
- CZ transfer logic: CZ construction is subcontractor-driven and suffers chronic labor shortage; sourcing of Ukrainian/Balkan crews is informal (Facebook groups, brokers) with zero vetting or compliance tooling.
- Absence check (2026-08-13): Tracxn CZ construction-tech lists and searches show no B2B subcontractor marketplace; only consumer-facing craftsmen marketplaces. Classification: weak incumbent (consumer-grade only).
