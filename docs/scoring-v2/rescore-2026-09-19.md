# Rescore worksheet, 2026-09-19

*The worklist for rescoring every live record to the money and urgency ladders the
owner approved on 2026-09-19 (`SCORING.md`, MONEY and URGENCY). **Nothing here is
applied.** No record was edited to make it. Agents apply it record by record in the
second pass, once the content edits finish.*

Read `SCORING.md` first. In one line each:

- **Why now (`urgency`, 0–3)** asks how close and how real the deadline is. It has no
  freshness point. Every trigger is a `regulation` source and passes three tests.
  REAL means enacted and binding on this buyer. CLOSE means the date is at most 18
  months after `updated`, or passed at most 12 months before it. TEETH means a named
  sanction, or enforcement on file for a date already past. The rungs: 0 no dated
  duty · 1 fails REAL or CLOSE · 2 REAL and CLOSE · 3 all three. A rule that only
  permits something, changes what the state pays, or sits in a plan with no bill
  scores 0.
- **Willing to pay (`money`, 0–2)** asks whether someone is paying for this job now.
  The only receipt is a `type: price` source tagged `dims: [money]`. The rungs: 0 no
  receipt (public money alone scores here) · 1 an asking price (list price,
  buyer's figure, cost by hand, or a paid receipt older than 24 months) · 2 a paid
  receipt within 24 months (signed contract, awarded tender line, a consultant paid
  to do it by hand), or rung 1 plus the public-money lift.
- **Unchanged:** proof, demand, gap, the max of 12 and the bands (10–12 PRIME · 8–9
  STRONG · 5–7 FAIR · 0–4 FAINT). Execution difficulty stays out of the total.

Every window is measured from **2026-09-19**, the date the rescoring agent writes into
`updated`: CLOSE runs to 2028-03-19 and back to 2025-09-19, and PAID to 2024-09-19.

## How to apply one record

1. Change `scores.urgency`, `scores.money` and `score` as in the table, and make the
   source edits listed under the record's **To apply**. Tag price receipts with
   `dims: [money]`, restate a contract or award for this job as `type: price`, and
   fix `dims` on sources whose old tag would now mislead.
2. Set `updated: 2026-09-19` (or the day you apply it).
3. Replace every `[Competition](#competition)` link in the body and the moves with
   `[Market gap](#competition)`. 32 records carry these links (65 in all).
4. Re-read the Why now and Willing to pay prose against the new numbers (MATCH §10:
   scores and prose are one artifact). Fix stale source `note:` lines that name the
   retired rungs ("freshness", "deadline sub-score", "money 2 on the open tender").
5. Write a Revisions entry dated today: `rescored to the 2026-09-19 ladders`, with old
   → new for each moved score and the evidence behind it.
6. Prove it: `python3 scripts/check-records.py --strict --enforce-scoring p-XXXX`
   must report 0 errors.
7. In the **same change**, add the id to `SCORING_V2_ENFORCED` in
   `scripts/check-records.py` **and** to `SCORING_V2` in `web/lib/scoring-v2.ts`.
   The checker fails the build if the two lists differ.

## Follow-ups outside the records

- **Freshness code.** For a rescored record, `web/lib/data.ts` `urgencySplit` already
  returns freshness 0, so `dimRefs` attaches no freshness ref and the page shows no
  "+1 for evidence under 90 days old". The SQLite port has no switch: it still
  computes freshness in `scripts/db.py` `urgency_split`, `dim_refs` and the
  `urgency_deadline` / `deadline_unbacked` views. This affects only the audit, not
  the rendered HTML, and the audit only gets looser for rescored records, which
  `URGENCY_NO_INSTRUMENT` covers. When the last record joins, delete freshness there
  and rebuild.
- **Bill-led records without `draft_law:`:** p-0027, p-0028, p-0044 and p-0045. Adding the key
  where the main pain rests on the bill would let `URGENCY_DRAFT` hold them. That is
  a headline decision (it shows the badge), so it is the owner's call.
- **The price unit vocabulary** has no per-kWh unit, so p-0001's price list (S18)
  cannot be a receipt.
- **p-0010 and p-0028:** a Czech list price, or a lift whose conditions hold, is one
  receipt away from money 2. Worth a targeted search in the next scan.
