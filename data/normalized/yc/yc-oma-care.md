- Model: tech platform that helps family caregivers access state caregiving money, get trained and get support; CareOasis (YC S23) is the same model — a validated US cluster.
- CZ transfer logic: příspěvek na péči (four levels, raised again in 2024-25) flows to ~380k dependent persons, largely spent on informal family care; application, hodnocení stupně závislosti and appeals are bureaucratic and opaque — exactly the navigation layer Oma Care monetizes.
- Absence check (2026-08-13): searches return only advice articles and government pages explaining the benefit; no startup that files, tracks or optimizes claims for families. Classification: no CZ player found (info portals only).
