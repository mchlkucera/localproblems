- Application date 10 Jul 2027 verified on EUR-Lex (Art 90: applies from 10 July 2027, football sector 2029); part of AML package with AMLD6 and AMLA Regulation (AMLA operational in Frankfurt since 2025).
- Directly applicable — replaces much of the Czech AML Act regime; new obliged entities (crowdfunding, most crypto services, luxury goods traders) enter scope.
- Market: gap analyses against AMLR vs. zákon 253/2008, UBO verification APIs, transaction monitoring upgrades, AMLA-ready reporting.
