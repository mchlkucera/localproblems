- Deadline basis: implementing acts adopted/published Nov-Dec 2024, wallets due 24 months after entry into force, i.e. end of December 2026; Commission EUDI page confirms "each Member State will offer at least one wallet ... by 2026" and launch "at the end of 2026" (receipt: https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/display/EUDIGITALIDENTITYWALLET/EU+Digital+Identity+Wallet+Home). Exact day not fixed publicly — treated as end-2026.
- Relying-party acceptance obligations (regulated sectors must accept the wallet where strong user authentication is required) follow within 36 months of implementing acts, i.e. during 2027.
- CZ angle: builds on eDoklady; banks, utilities, e-shops with KYC, and municipalities need wallet-acceptance flows.
- Market: relying-party registration and integration SDKs, KYC-flow rebuilds, QES/attribute attestation services for CZ businesses.
