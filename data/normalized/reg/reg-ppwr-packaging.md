- Entered into force 11 Feb 2025; applies from 12 Aug 2026 (18 months later), with staggered later deadlines (e.g. recyclability grading from 2030, reuse targets 2030/2040). Verified via EUR-Lex and Gleiss Lutz summary (https://www.gleisslutz.com/en/know-how/new-eu-packaging-regulation-key-requirements-august-2026).
- Directly effective regulation — no CZ transposition lag; replaces packaging directive regime, affects CZ e-commerce, food producers, FMCG and their EPR reporting to EKO-KOM.
- Market: packaging portfolio audits, PFAS testing, empty-space ratio compliance for e-commerce, EPR/PPWR data platforms.
