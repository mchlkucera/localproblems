- Deadline: 2027-07-09 (<18 months) — authorities must accept eFTI/eCMR; hauliers that digitize first cut roadside-check friction and paperwork cost.
- Market created: certified eFTI platforms, eCMR services, TMS integrations, qualified trust services (seals/timestamps).
- Relevance: deadline receipt for p-0010 (trucking back-office) — the problem gains a regulatory trigger it lacked.
