- Verified timeline as of Aug 2026: prohibitions applied 2 Feb 2025; GPAI obligations applied 2 Aug 2025; 2 Aug 2026 = general application incl. Art 50 transparency (Art 50(2) watermarking grace period for existing systems until 2 Dec 2026).
- Digital Omnibus on AI: provisional political agreement 6 May 2026, confirmed by Member States 13 May 2026 — postpones high-risk deadlines to 2 Dec 2027 (Annex III stand-alone) and 2 Aug 2028 (Annex I embedded). Binding only upon OJ publication, expected before 2 Aug 2026 (receipt: https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/).
- CZ angle: national supervisory structure and sandbox obligations; CZ SMEs deploying chatbots/gen-AI in customer-facing roles hit Art 50 first.
