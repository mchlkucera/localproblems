- Non-eurozone deadlines verified: receive instant EUR payments 9 Jan 2027; send during business hours + VoP 9 Jul 2027; send outside business hours 9 Jun 2028 (receipt: https://worldline.com/en/home/main-navigation/resources/blogs/2025/instant-payments-regulation-a-key-development-for-non-eurozone-eu-countries). Eurozone PSPs already live (Jan/Oct 2025).
- CZ angle: CZK-centric banks and payment institutions must build SEPA Instant rails and name-IBAN checking; smaller PSPs/EMIs lack in-house capability.
- Market: VoP API providers, instant-payment gateway integration, real-time AML/sanctions screening, liquidity management for 24/7 settlement.
