**1. Approve p-0050's title, brief, solution and good_for.**
Title: *"Czech towns and firms that got a grant can be made to pay part of it back over one
wrongly run tender."* **Recommend: approve the title and brief. Reword the solution.** It says
*"as 2 companies already do in 2 other countries"*, but AmpliFund tracks recipient compliance and
does not check tenders, and Najda is a consultancy. "Similar services exist in the US and Poland"
would be honest. The brief's "about 374,000 CZK each on average" is our own division
(365.3M / 977), and Revisions flags it.

turns on counting procurement administrators as selling *this*: SCORING calls "a service firm
rather than a product vendor" adjacent, while the three price receipts buy exactly that service.
**Recommend: publish as `watching`.** The pain and the paid receipts are the best-measured of the
week, and the page says honestly that the space is taken.
