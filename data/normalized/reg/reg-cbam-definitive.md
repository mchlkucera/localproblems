- Verified via EY alert on adopted CBAM Omnibus: definitive regime from 1 Jan 2026; 50-ton per-importer annual de minimis exempts ~90% of importers while keeping ~99% of emissions in scope; certificate sales from Feb 2027; first declaration due 30 Sep 2027 for 2026 reporting year (receipt: https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/eu-adopts-cbam-omnibus-regulation).
- CZ angle: metal fabrication, machinery and construction importers of steel/aluminium inputs; CFO-level cash planning for certificate liability accruing since Jan 2026.
- Market: CBAM declarant registration support, supplier emissions data collection, ERP plug-ins for embedded emissions, hedging advisory.
