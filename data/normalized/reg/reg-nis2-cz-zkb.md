- Act No. 264/2025 Coll. adopted 26 Jun 2025, effective 1 Nov 2025; replaces Act 181/2014. Two regimes (lower/higher obligations) per implementing decrees (e.g. Vyhláška 409/2025 for higher regime).
- Self-registration of regulated services with NÚKIB was due within 60 days of effectiveness (~31 Dec 2025); security measures must be implemented within 1 year of registration — most deadlines land Q4 2026 – H1 2027.
- SME UNION reports 6,000+ firms affected across energy, manufacturing, food, logistics, digital services; many SMEs still unaware (receipt: https://www.sme-union.cz/zakon-o-kyberneticke-bezpecnosti-plati-lhuty-bezi/).
- Market: gap analyses, ISMS implementation, incident-reporting workflows, supplier-chain security clauses, municipal IT hardening.
