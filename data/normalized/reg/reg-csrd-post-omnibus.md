- Status verified Aug 2026: Stop-the-clock Directive (Apr 2025) already deferred wave 2/3; Dec 2025 trilogue agreement sets scope at >1,000 employees + EUR 450m turnover, and both wave 1 (exempted for FY2025-26) and wave 2 first report on FY2027, published 2028 (receipt: https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2025/12/eu-omnibus-package-trilogue-agreement-on-eu-csrd-and-csddd). Final Omnibus I text pending formal adoption — treat scope/dates as agreed but not yet in OJ.
- Date caveat: no single EU-wide filing day; reports due with 2028 annual reports (member-state timing). Date 2028-01-01 marks start of the reporting cycle, not a hard deadline.
- CZ angle: scope now limited to largest CZ corporates (banks, utilities, industrial groups), but their value-chain data requests cascade to CZ SME suppliers regardless (VSME standard).
- Market: ESRS-lite/VSME reporting tools for suppliers, CO2 accounting, assurance preparation.
