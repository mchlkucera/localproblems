- Status: obligation live since 2025-06-28; enforcement wave 2026 (ČOI test purchases); self-service terminals transition until 2030.
- Who: CZ e-shops, banks, transport ticketing, e-book sellers, consumer-device makers (micro-enterprise service exemption only).
- Market created: WCAG 2.1 audits and remediation, automated accessibility monitoring SaaS, accessibility-statement tooling.
- Additional sources: https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2023-424 ; https://www.shop5.cz/clanek/faq-pristupnost-e-shopu-a-zakon-c-424-2023-sb-na-koho-se-skutecne-vztahuje/
