**A. AI-literacy measurement under the AI Act** (`gb-academyai`) — **DO NOT CREATE.**
Two independent reasons. (1) **Aibility, s. r. o., IČO 10684395, since 2021**, already sells exactly

**B. Outcome-blindness in Czech research funding** (`nku-zemedelsky-vyzkum`) — **DO NOT CREATE as a
funder record.** Demand would be a genuine 2 — three NKÚ audits of one defect across three funders
(24/17 GAČR/TAČR, 25/13 MZd, 25/18 MZe). The buyer is the defect: audit 25/13 records that MZd

Seven years of an explicit government order, unbought — documented non-purchase, not a hesitant
buyer. Urgency 0, money 0. The judge's counter-proposal is in decision 9.

**C. Ombudsman administrative-practice methodology** (`ombud-spravni-rad-praxe`) — **DO NOT CREATE.**
The best-measured demand in the haul (1,422 returns, ±4.0 pts at 95%) and still refused: what the
offices ask for is a **free** methodology from the Interior Ministry, which **already publishes
"Metodické pomůcky ke správnímu řádu"**, while the paid version is sold to the same towns by
Wolters Kluwer (ASPI, IČO 63077639, since 1995) and ATLAS consulting (CODEXIS, IČO 46578706, since
1992) — both `direct` + `established`, through the Svaz měst a obcí channel. Gap 0. No sourced loss
in crowns, hours or complaints anywhere in the survey.

**D. Czech public tenders that have to be re-run** — **DO NOT CREATE, and reject permanently.**
Counted rather than assumed: **5 genuine re-runs in 195 notices** (2.6%), not a pattern hiding under
the surface. Four different causes under one shape (no bids, no compliant bid, subsidy didn't arrive,
special regard) — each a different product. No Czech source puts a crown or a month figure on what
one cancelled procurement costs one buyer. And MATCH.md §9 rule 2 names this exact case: re-running a
tender is how the register *sees* problems, not a problem. **p-0031 works only because the object is
identical across towns, so it can be pooled; generalise and you delete the pooling.**

> **A counting trap worth keeping.** 46 of the 195 notices carry a "Republication candidate" note —
