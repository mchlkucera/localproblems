- Deadlines: 2026-09-12 (access by design — one month away) and 2027-01-12 (switching charges) — both <18 months.
- Who: CZ IoT/device manufacturers (industrial equipment, appliances, vehicles), all cloud/SaaS providers selling in the EU.
- Market created: device data-sharing APIs and consent management, contract remediation, cloud-exit/portability tooling.
