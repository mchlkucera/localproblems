- Date verified: applies from 20 Jan 2027, hard cutover — machines placed on market from that date must conform to the Regulation, not Directive 2006/42/EC (receipts: https://www.tuv.com/world/en/new-machinery-regulation-eu-2023-1230.html, https://www.pilz.com/en-US/support/law-standards-norms/manufacturer-machine-operators/machinery-regulation).
- Highly relevant for CZ: machinery/engineering is a core CZ export sector; "substantial modification" rule turns integrators and retrofitters into manufacturers with full conformity duties.
- Market: conformity gap audits, safety-related control system (incl. cybersecurity per Annex III 1.1.9/1.2.1) assessments, digital user-instruction platforms.
