## Decisions for you

1. **Publish p-0046?** Its title, brief, solution and good_for are all new and need your approval. **Recommend: yes.** The first headline claimed an electric bus costs "more than twice" a diesel one. That rests on a 2023 price, and 2026 tenders are about 1.7×, so I rewrote it before committing.
2. **p-0046 Willing to pay = 2.** It rests on outright purchases of buses and chargers (Transdev and MAN 75.4M CZK, Opava, Liberec, Ostrava), read as the do-it-yourself version of a fee per bus. **Recommend: accept.** The product still pays for the buses, just over time.
3. **p-0046 Why now = 2.** This reads the 60% clean-bus share as a duty that started on 1 Jan 2026. If you read it as due at the end of the period in 2030, it is 1. A fine is named, but there is no enforcement on file, so not 3. **Recommend: accept 2.**
4. **The founding-year floor for comparables is 1980** (`CompSchema.since` in `web/lib/data.ts`). It kept Deutsche Leasing (founded 1962, finances 85 e-buses with their chargers in Lübeck) out of p-0046's comparables, so proof is 2, not 3. **Recommend: lower the floor** (it is a schema bug for old firms, per MATCH §2). Then re-add Deutsche Leasing and let proof go back to 3, giving a score of 11. Counting a finance-only company as proof is itself a judgement, so say no if you think it isn't the full model.
5. **p-0028 draft-law badge.** Act 159/2026 is passed, applies to every e-shop from 1 Jan 2027 and names an offence, so it passes all three Why-now tests. The template's check ("take the draft away — does Why now still stand?") now says yes. **Recommend: remove the badge**, which moves urgency 1 → 3 and the score 8 → 10. Keep it only if you want the eco-claims bill to stay the headline pain.
6. **p-0035 brief.** Proposed: "Hospitals buy most medicine on their own; two bought the same cancer drug separately in September 2026 [S1,S20,S21]. A state audit found one paying over 3 times what another paid for the same antibiotic [S7]." Some university hospitals now pool single drugs, so "each hospital buys on its own" is no longer quite true. **Recommend: approve.** Check the S numbers after the merge.
7. **p-0035 revision entry.** Folding the four 2026-09-19 entries left one entry of about 800 words, because nothing could be dropped (it includes texts kept word for word). **Recommend: accept it**, or allow the word-for-word old texts to move into a source note.

9. **p-0022 demand stays 1.** You could argue for 2: Semily's amendment blames the state e-health centre, on top of the state audit and the contract-change churn. **Recommend: keep 1** until a second hospital complains.

The rules say only Czech players count. **Recommend: count any vendor actively selling in Czechia as a local.** Write that into CONVENTIONS with an invariant in `check-records.py`.
