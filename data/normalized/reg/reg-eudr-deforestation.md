- Second postponement adopted Dec 2025: Parliament vote 11 Dec 2025, Council/EP deal 4 Dec 2025 — application 30 Dec 2026 for large/medium companies, 30 Jun 2027 for micro/small; simplifications: only first placer on EU market files DDS, one-off simplified declaration for micro/small primary operators, printed products excluded (receipt: https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20251211IPR32168/deforestation-law-parliament-adopts-changes-to-postpone-and-simplify-measures).
- CZ angle: furniture/wood processing, coffee roasters, food producers, tyre/rubber importers; heavy documentation burden on mid-sized importers with no traceability stack.
- Market: EUDR due-diligence SaaS, geolocation plot verification, supplier questionnaire automation, customs-integration tooling.
